# How this was built

Kevin designed the authored pharmacy conversations and ranking rules. His September 2026 source checks remain historical author-review stamps. AI coding tools assisted implementation, tests and this proof package.

The current passage mapping and 12 development plus 20 final evaluation lines are AI-proposed drafts. Kevin has not reviewed their labels or approved the new support mapping. They must not be described as human-authored benchmark evidence. No independent second reviewer has evaluated them.

The engine follows fixed choices and flags. It generates no dialogue. The ranking combines the authored outcome with listening, permission and pressure. An exhaustive walk evaluates 37,646 finished paths across the five trees. This proves properties of those authored rules, not effects on real people.

Claim, passage and dialogue-use hashes use SHA-256 of JSON-serialized values in UTF-8. Source-response and media hashes use raw bytes.

The November 1 bindings anchor the audited public-main content. They are not cryptographic signatures created on the historical September review dates.

The offline proof exporter inventories every tree string and exports all 24 claims with exact retrieved CDC narrative passages, source dates where present, retrieval dates, source-response hashes, rights notes and historical content bindings. A second inclusive inventory captures source string literals, template fragments and JSX text throughout the current counter application, including UI and scene labels. Classification of those surfaces as factual or ordinary remains pending human review.

Retrieval uses ordinary public HTTP access. No CAPTCHA, account or access restriction was bypassed. The old COVID source is a meta redirect. Its current destination was retrieved separately and flagged for correction review rather than silently approving changed guidance. Historical summarized research files are preserved and are not represented as exact primary-source captures.

CDC narrative excerpts are attributed to CDC. Federal text is generally public domain in the US, with exceptions for third-party material. Excerpt-level rights decisions remain draft. CDC materials are available free at the linked originals. Use of these materials and links does not imply CDC, HHS or US government endorsement. No CDC logo is used. See the [CDC use policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html).

Source anchors are user-activated links in the debrief. They use a new tab with no opener or referrer. No request is needed to play. The bundle scanner rejects automatic networking and remote embedded resources, with a content security policy as another barrier.

Offline regeneration creates content-addressed report directories and refuses to replace differing historical files. The fixed-rule gallery selects unresolved support findings before pressure ranking, missing source and inaccessible choice findings, then other gate errors, with stable ID order. The full error list stays available alongside it.

Automated browser runs verify layout and keyboard behavior. Screenshots and GIF frames are captured from this public candidate. They are not observations from human participants. The three-player pilot is prepared in [PILOT.md](PILOT.md) and remains pending.
